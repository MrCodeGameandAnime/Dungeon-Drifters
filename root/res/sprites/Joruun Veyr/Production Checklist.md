## Verification

- Verified dimensions, alpha, padding, and active content in every PNG cell. All 24 intentionally clear cells have zero alpha. Inspected the artwork on light and dark backgrounds and checked staff silhouettes, costume palette, hand placement, and pose progression.
- New character sheets share the idle’s measured contact line after allowing for atlas padding. The contact rows are 505 for 576-pixel cells, 537 for 640-pixel cells and 553 for 672-pixel cells. The approved idle retains its existing one-pixel variation on frames 5–6. Reused idle keys match the original frame pixel for pixel.
- Verified matching start keys for both consumption fades and the optional Super aftermath. Inspected the overlapping blue and green setup states, the gold-dominant fusion, and the separate Super flight and impact in composite samples.
- All 17 timed GIFs decoded. Review crops were inspected from the encoded GIFs. **Sprite Pop and in-game review are pending.** [Animation Timings](Animation%20Timings.md)
