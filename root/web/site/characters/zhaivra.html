Zhaivra Kelyth

The Uncontrolled Reagent

An Ashvein surveyor from a living homeland, with a perfect eye, a prepared compound for every problem, and no patience for waste.

HomelandNhal Voryss
RoleAlchemical Marksman
WeaponSathren
DisciplineAshvein Alchemy
“An arrow can strike exactly where you intended and still become the worst mistake of your life.”

The ashvein

From the ever-burning arteries of Nhal Voryss comes an archer trained to read air currents through mineral dust and identify combustible gases by the way they bend distant light.

She once served the Ashvein Survey, maintaining the volatile passages of the Hollow Colossus, the immense petrified organism within which her people built their civilization.

How she fights

Zhaivra is precise, analytical, and relentlessly prepared. Her bow Sathren carries six internal reservoirs, allowing a rotating thumb-ring to coat each arrow moments before release.

Fire and Poison Infused Barbs turn preparation into pressure. She rarely chooses the most destructive shot when a more efficient one will do the work.

Why the dungeon

She found traces of bone beneath a ruined surface monastery that looked like the Hollow Colossus. Something buried below may explain what her homeland truly is and whether the First Turning can ever be undone.
