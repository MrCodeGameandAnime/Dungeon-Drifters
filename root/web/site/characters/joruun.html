Joruun Veyr

The Bloody Storm Monk

A wandering Veyrathi martial artist, part miracle worker and part professional nuisance, who treats the weather as a second weapon.

HomelandKharuun Shelf
RoleElemental Skirmisher
WeaponSky-Needle
DisciplineLaw of the Single Current
“The heavens provide the rain, the earth provides the grain, and the brewery provides salvation. Amen.”

The prophet

From the storm-beaten Kharuun Shelf comes a monk of questionable holiness. Joruun wanders village to village performing genuine miracles for donations, free drinks, and the attention of beautiful women.

Beneath the robes, dark spectacles, and rehearsed wisdom is a gifted martial artist with an equally impressive talent for gambling, deception, and getting thrown out of taverns.

How he fights

Joruun switches between Water, Air, and Lightning through the Law of the Single Current. Rain, rivers, blood, and charged skies can turn a careful technique into something far more dangerous.

He is mobile, deceptive, and adaptable, chaining martial pressure with Stun and rapid elemental flow.

Why the dungeon

He claims to be pursuing a sacred Veyrathi matter. His explanation changes depending on who asks, how much they are paying, and whether the nearest tavern is still open.
