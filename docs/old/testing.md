**LOCAL ONLY: do not commit, publish, or reference this note in public documentation.**

After pressing **Start**, open the Drifter picker and press `Shift+Alt+C` to enter a test code. The shortcut is unavailable before Start and outside the picker. Codes are case-insensitive; invalid codes leave the current state unchanged.

| Code | Starting state |
| --- | --- |
| `H01B` | Branoc at the Floor 1 Hub before companion recruitment, with 75 shared gold. |
| `F01BA` | Branoc and Azhvielle at the Hub after a full Floor 1 clear. The tutorial is complete, party resources are restored, and Floor 1 rewards and drops are secured. Current authored payouts yield 106 shared gold, 30 Goblin Scrap, and 1 Goblin Lord Core. |
