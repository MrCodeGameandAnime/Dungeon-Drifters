# Code of Conduct

Dungeon Drifters is a collaborative project. We want this community to be
welcoming, respectful, and useful for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code in good faith.
- Welcome questions, feedback, and different perspectives.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a respectful creative community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
Dungeon Drifters maintainers through GitHub's private contact options. Please
include what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant links or evidence.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action
when necessary to protect the community. Decisions will consider the facts,
context, and safety of the people involved.

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and activities where someone is
representing Dungeon Drifters or participating in its community.
