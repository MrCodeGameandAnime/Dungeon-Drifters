The loop

Choose. Commit. Continue.

Enter an encounter, manage the resources you brought with you, and make room for the next fight. Rest points restore HP and Mana, but the road only moves forward when you decide it should.

  • Eight authored surface encounters
  • Single and multi-enemy battles
  • Three one-time Rest points
  • Save, quit, load, and continue
