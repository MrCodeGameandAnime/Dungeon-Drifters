Ser Branoc

The Unbroken Crest

A knight without a crest, carrying a great blade forged from broken Deep-Iron and the weight of every soldier he could not save.

HomelandRhom-Ghal
RoleHeavy Vanguard
WeaponSunder-Spire
DisciplineRhom-Ghalian Lung
“Endurance is merely suffering with direction.”

The sentinel

From the Iron-Spires of Rhom-Ghal comes a knight who surrendered his name and rank to spare the soldiers under his command. He now walks the outer kingdoms in scarred Deep-Iron plate, accepting provisions and information instead of gold.

Behind the narrow grille of his helm is no immortal or revenant. Branoc is flesh and blood, sustained by a punishing mountain discipline that lets him fight through thin air, smoke, exhaustion, and pain.

How he fights

Branoc plants himself in the fight and makes the enemy pay for every step forward. Brace turns defense into a promise, while Ironwake Dismemberment answers that promise with crushing force.

He is durable, relentless, and slow: a close-range fighter built around endurance, retaliation, guard breaking, and controlled pressure.

Why the dungeon

He seeks a lost mark of the Third Gate, hoping it will lead him to the scattered remnants of his fallen order. He does not know what waits beneath the stone, but he has already decided to meet it standing.
